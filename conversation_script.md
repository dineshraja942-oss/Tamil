# Bilingual Conversation Script
## Format: English Speaker (English) → [15 sec gap] → LOTE Speaker (Pronunciation)

---

### Exchange 1

**English Speaker:**
Good afternoon. My name is John Smith. How can I help you today?

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Madhiya vanakkam, Thirumathi Smith. Maruththuvamanaiyilirundhu marundhu seettu (Rx) moolam marundhugalai edukka vendiyulladhu. Enakku udalnala kaappeedu ulladhu; aanaal adhan selavai adhu cover seiyaadhu.

---

### Exchange 2

**English Speaker:**
Firstly we advise you to talk to insurer directly. If policy you have cover for rx, its their duty by law to cover everything.

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Aam, naan en udalnala kaappeedu niruvanaththudan pesivitten. Naan Australian kudimagan illai endraal, Medicare selavugalai cover seiyaadhu endru avargal koorinaar.

---

### Exchange 3

**English Speaker:**
I see. They did not tell you the exact expense?

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Illai, avargal kooravillai. Maruththuvamanai moththa selavai enakku theriviththadhu; adhu migavum adhigamaaga ulladhu.

---

### Exchange 4

**English Speaker:**
Well, yes, hospital are free to charge any amount to private client.

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Udalnala kaappeedu niruvanam ennai yemaatrivittadhu. Naan Australianar allaadhadhaal, thittamittadhai vida adhiga thogai seluththa vendiyulladhu.

---

### Exchange 5

**English Speaker:**
As they did not explain you properly, you can lodge a claim in the court.

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Naan oru vazhakkaRignarai niyamikkka vendumaa?

---

### Exchange 6

**English Speaker:**
No. We can directly contact the company and ask for the reason. You want us to do that?

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Sari. Adhai neengal seiyalaam.

---

### Exchange 7

**English Speaker:**
There is no guarantee that they will increase the amount which is covered by your plan. But they have to explain us, and hopefully it will change the future.

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
Adhu nadakkum endru nambugiren. Idhu enakku neradiyaaga payan aLikkaamal irukkalaam; aanaal ethirkaala vaadikkaiyaalargalukku udhaviyaaga irukkum.

---

### Exchange 8

**English Speaker:**
We hope so it change. It depends mostly upon customer action and demands. We are here to help you.

*[15 second gap]*

**LOTE Speaker:**
*(No further LOTE response in this exchange)*

---
